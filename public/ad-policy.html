Advertising content policy

These initial rules help keep Shivasha advertising useful, transparent, and safer for members. Campaigns are subject to manual review and may be rejected or paused.

Current MVP notice: Shivasha Ad Network is in an early stage. Campaign budgets are planning values only. This portal does not collect advertising payments or promise impressions, clicks, sales, or leads. Review is manual and supported placements may be limited.

1. Advertiser responsibilities

  • Provide accurate business identity and truthful, clear ad claims.
  • Use a destination website that you own or are authorized to promote, and ensure it works securely over HTTPS.
  • Obtain permission for logos, photos, music, testimonials, and other content you submit.
  • Follow applicable Indian laws, consumer-protection rules, sector-specific requirements, and platform policies.
  • Do not mislead users about prices, results, discounts, earnings, qualifications, or product availability.

2. Content that is not allowed

  • Fraud, phishing, impersonation, pyramid schemes, deceptive investment promises, or guaranteed-income claims.
  • Illegal products or services, stolen goods, or instructions to evade laws.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, exploitation, sexual abuse, or content that targets people for protected characteristics.
  • Malware, spyware, credential theft, deceptive downloads, or malicious redirects.
  • False medical claims, dangerous treatment advice, or unverified claims that a product cures serious disease.
  • Ads that collect sensitive personal information without a lawful basis and appropriate notice/consent.
  • Content that infringes another person's intellectual-property or privacy rights.

Shivasha may reject content or destinations that appear unsafe, misleading, unlawful, or inconsistent with these rules.

3. Targeting and privacy

  • Do not target or exploit people based on sensitive personal data.
  • Do not upload another person's contact lists or personal data without proper permission and a lawful basis.
  • Advertisers are responsible for their own landing-page privacy notices, consent, and data handling.
  • Shivasha's initial ad slot uses the selected campaign's format and displays a “Sponsored” label. Advanced profiling and retargeting are not part of this MVP.

4. Review, rejection, and appeals

Campaigns begin as drafts. Advertisers must submit them for review. Only approved campaigns are eligible for the supported Shivasha Social placement. A rejection may include a review note. Edit the campaign or contact Shivasha support before resubmitting; review does not guarantee delivery.

5. Budget, metrics, and payments

The daily budget and duration currently represent an estimate supplied by the advertiser. The current MVP does not charge the amount, take payment, issue advertising invoices, or provide a verified billing ledger. Basic impression/click counters are not independently audited and must not be treated as guaranteed reach or billable proof. Shivasha must announce separate terms before enabling paid campaigns.

6. Enforcement and reporting

Shivasha may pause, reject, or remove ads and restrict accounts where there is a credible policy or safety concern. Users may report a suspicious ad through the available Shivasha support/contact channel. Include the ad name, the issue, and relevant evidence; do not send passwords or payment credentials.

7. Policy updates and contact

These are initial rules for the early-stage platform, not a substitute for legal advice or a complete advertising contract. Before monetization, Shivasha should publish finalized Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, payment/refund terms, complaint handling, and applicable business contact details.
